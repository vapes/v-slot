Dragon's Nest

{{symbol:egg}} — SCATTER
3 or more Eggs anywhere wake the mother dragon and start the Dragon's Nest respins.
  1. Night falls. The dragon's fire burns away the low cards: only Eggs, Wild, Dragon Medal, Treasure Chest, Gold Hoard, Crystal and Goblet remain on the reels.
  2. Every Egg is sticky: it stays in the nest and carries a cash prize from 1× to 25× the bet, or a MINI / MAJOR jackpot.
  3. You get 3 respins. Every new Egg resets them back to 3.
  4. Line wins still pay on every respin.
  5. When the respins run out, all Eggs hatch and pay their prizes.
  6. Fill all 20 positions with Eggs to win the GRAND HOARD — 1000× the bet on top.
