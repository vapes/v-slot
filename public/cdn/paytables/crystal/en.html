Symbols pay anywhere on the screen: 8 or more of the same symbol win. Values are shown for the current bet of {{bet}}.

Tumble

  1. After every win the winning symbols burst, the symbols above fall down and new ones drop in from the top.
  2. Tumbling continues while new wins appear — one spin can win many times.
  3. {{symbol:scatter}} never bursts and stays until the tumbling ends.

Power Orb

Each {{symbol:mult}} carries a random multiplier from ×2 to ×500. When the tumbling ends and the spin won, all orbs on the screen add up and multiply the total tumble win.

Free spins

4 or more {{symbol:scatter}} anywhere after the tumbling pay {{mult:scatter:4}} / {{mult:scatter:5}} / {{mult:scatter:6}} the bet (4 / 5 / 6+) and start 15 free spins.
  • In free spins every orb of a winning spin adds to a total multiplier that multiplies every following win.
  • 3 or more {{symbol:scatter}} in free spins add +5 free spins.

Feature buy

Press {{t:button.buy}} (left of the win box) to start the free spins right away for {{buyCost}} — 135× the current bet. The bought spin always lands 4 or more {{symbol:scatter}}.
